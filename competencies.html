UN COMPETENCY FRAMEWORK

UN Core Values & Managerial Competencies

Core pillars assessed during written supervisory scenarios (AMS) and the Competency-Based Interview (CBI).

1. UN Core Values

Integrity

Uphold the values of the United Nations in daily activities and behaviors. Act without consideration of personal gain, resist undue political pressure, and promptly report abuse, fraud, or misconduct.

Focus: Zero Tolerance SEA / Ethics

Professionalism

Show pride in work and achievements; demonstrate professional competence and mastery of subject matter; remain conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments and deadlines; stay calm under pressure.

Focus: Composure Under Stress

Respect for Diversity

Work effectively with people from all backgrounds; treat all individuals with dignity and respect; examine own biases to avoid stereotypical responses; never discriminate by gender, religion, or nationality.

Focus: Multicultural Teams

2. Key Supervisory & Management Competencies (AMS)

Establish clear goals for the team, delegate responsibilities fairly, provide timely constructive feedback, and empower subordinates while maintaining clear accountability.

Identify key issues in complex operational incidents, gather relevant facts before acting, assess risks and consequences, and make firm, proportionate decisions even under pressure or with incomplete information.

Speak and write clearly and effectively in English; listen actively to others; tailor language to the audience; place team agenda before personal agenda and share credit for accomplishments.

Develop clear goals consistent with agreed strategies; identify priority activities and assignments; adjust priorities as required; allocate appropriate time, shifts, and resources fairly.
