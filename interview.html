COMPETENCY-BASED INTERVIEW (CBI)

Sample Interview Questions & STAR Stories

Common UN AMS Interview Questions
  • Judgement / Decision-Making: "Tell me about a time you had to make an immediate decision in a high-risk operational situation without waiting for instructions."
  • Leadership: "Describe a situation where you had to lead a team under intense pressure and keep everyone safe and focused."
  • Integrity: "Give an example of a time when you witnessed a breach of protocol or unethical conduct. What action did you take?"
  • Planning & Organizing: "Tell me about a time you designed or adjusted a shift schedule or resource plan to ensure fairness and operational readiness."
  • Respect for Diversity: "Describe a situation where you worked with people from different backgrounds or resolved a conflict within a team."
